🎮 HOW TO PLAY

🧱 Block Buster Puzzle

1️⃣ Select Block

Choose any block from the bottom area.

2️⃣ Drag & Drop

Drag the block and place it on the 8×8 board.

3️⃣ Clear Lines

Complete rows or columns to clear blocks and earn points.

Clear multiple lines to increase your combo and get bonus score.
